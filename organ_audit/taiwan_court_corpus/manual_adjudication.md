## Evidence boundary

The Changhua records establish a paid cross-border transplant pathway but not the identity, consent, custody status, or death pathway of any donor. The Southeast Asia judgments establish documented communications concerning threatened organ removal but not that an organ was actually removed or transplanted.

A positive post-2015 prisoner-sourcing finding still requires an independently corroborated chain connecting an identifiable person in custody to compatibility testing, death or disappearance, procurement, allocation or bypass, and a recipient operation.
